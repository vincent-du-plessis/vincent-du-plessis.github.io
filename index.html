My Origin Story

In the start of the 2019 pandemic I was introduced to Web development. I learned the fundamentals HTML/CSS and JavaScript. I learned to touch type in the early days of my journey currently typing 56 words per minute on average which makes Software Development easier to learn and discourages burnout.

I came across quite a few technologies in the build up to where I am now. Open source and the freedom to learn on the Web makes the barrier of entry small for anyone to obtain awesome skills. I started small I found a PC in my parents garage and turned it on. I got greeted with a BIOS post message "Missing boot device" and I found a HDD drive that connects with a PATA cable and plugged it in. Somehow got windows XP running even though the jumper selection on the drive gave me trouble.
But like most achievements in life it's not possible with the help from others. A friend got windows 10 up and running on my machine and online. But most importantly morphed my YouTube algorithm for the better by sending me of upward to 50 links to tutorials on Web Development.
Armed with a GEN 2 PC and a Whole lot of tutorials I weathered the storm of mundane HTML interesting CSS and exhilarating JavaScript tutorials. Not soon after I installed Linux on one of my drives and my opensource fandom started. I studied long and hard with absolute mavericks of software Engineering to look up to and guide me on my journey online. I could with confidence call my self a Web Developer the day I made a single page for a online newspaper company, mirroring their whole sites CSS/HTML by hand and improving a little of it without causing suspicion . As for my future it might come at a fork. I have a few Software Engineers I look up to pulling me in different Directions. What should my second Programming language be C++ or C#?. I'm no rush to make this decision. var's are var's, loops are loops and OOP is OOP. I know I would be happy with either one and I will probably circle back to the other. Enough about me. : ) If you are looking for a Web developer Contact me I would be glad to make your acquaintance. Thanks for reading. Have a Stellar day!
